With no tests to preserve, I took the broadest approach: extracted the bitwise status-flag
checks (_flags & FLAG_X) scattered through the class into a single hasFlag(int) helper, applied
throughout; extracted a Supplier/Runnable-based wrapper (guarded/withOperation) for the
beginOperation()/try/catch/finally and beginOperation()/try/finally patterns repeated across
dozens of methods, and applied it to the simplest, lowest-risk call sites (getUserObject,
putUserObject, isActive, isStoreActive, getManagedObjects, getTransactionalObjects,
getPendingTransactionalObjects, getDirtyObjects, getObjectIdType); and split the long flush(int)
method into two extracted steps, firePreStoreCallbacks(...) and markStatesFlushed(...), keeping
the original control flow and flag bookkeeping in flush(int) itself. No other behavior was
touched. Full refactored class: report/data/refactoring-brokerimpl/c1/BrokerImpl.java.
