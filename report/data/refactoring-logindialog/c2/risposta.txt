behavior the tests depend on (constructor side effects, widget tree structure, the exact
PopupPanel property round-trips, and the two callback inner classes). I extracted only the two
lowest-risk pieces of the constructor: applyStyleNames(table, label, header) for the four
addStyleName calls, and onEnterClicked(traderName) for the validation-and-submit logic that used
to live inline in the click handler's onClick(). The trader name text box stays a local variable
captured by the click handler closure, exactly as in the original, so no new field was
introduced. Everything else, including field declarations, the inner classes and their method
bodies, is unchanged. Full refactored class:
report/data/refactoring-logindialog/c2/LoginDialog.java.
